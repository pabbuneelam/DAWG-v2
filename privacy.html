Privacy

LIFESTYLE works without an account. If you don't sign in, everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

If you sign in

Your LIFESTYLE data is backed up to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so it survives a lost phone and stays in sync across your devices. That includes:

  • Your name, email address and sign-in method
  • Goals, ticked days, XP, rank, streaks, challenges and your 60-day program
  • Journal entries and mood
  • Tracker numbers such as steps, water, sleep, workout, calories and protein
  • Your to-do list

Your data can only be read or changed by you while signed in. It is not shared with anyone, sold, or used for ads.

Steps from Apple Health

LIFESTYLE never reads Apple Health directly. Only the daily step total that your own Shortcut copies and you import is stored.

Deleting your data

In LIFESTYLE go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently erases your cloud backup and sign-in. Data on your device stays until you use Reset everything.
